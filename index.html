WHY MORROW EXISTS

A small pause can make space for a next step.

Teen life can hold school pressure, changing friendships, tiredness and big feelings all at once. Morrow is a starting point for understanding common experiences and choosing one manageable thing to try.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your answers stay in this moment.

Morrow does not ask for a name, email, phone number, school or location. Answers are processed in your browser memory only. They are not stored by the website and are not sent to a server.

BANGLADESH SUPPORT

Reaching out is a strength.

If something feels hard to carry, consider speaking with a trusted adult, school counsellor or health professional.

16263 - Bangladesh government 24-hour health service hotline. Verify details
