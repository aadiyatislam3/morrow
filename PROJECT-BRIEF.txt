==================================================
PROJECT
==================================================

Create an educational teenage mental wellbeing website.

The website should help teenagers:

1. Learn about common mental wellbeing challenges.
2. Reflect on their own wellbeing through a short 5-question self-check.
3. Receive personalized, general coping suggestions based on their responses.
4. Find appropriate support if they feel they need it.

The website must feel like a REAL PRODUCT.

It should not look like:
- a school worksheet
- a basic HTML project
- a generic AI landing page
- a medical hospital website
- an AI therapist

It should feel calm, trustworthy, modern and genuinely useful to a teenager.

--------------------------------------------------
RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------

The result should NOT simply be:

"Your score is 7."

That is too generic.

Instead identify the areas where the user reported the most difficulty.

For example:

If stress is high:

"Stress seems to be one of the areas affecting you most right now."

Then show relevant suggestions.

If sleep is high:

"Sleep seems to be an area worth paying attention to."

Then show sleep suggestions.

If stress + sleep are high:

"Stress and sleep seem to be the areas affecting you most right now."

Then show suggestions for both.

The result should therefore change depending on the user's actual answers.

--------------------------------------------------
SUGGESTION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------

Create a JavaScript mapping between each wellbeing area and appropriate general suggestions.

Example:

MOOD

- keep a manageable routine
- spend time doing something you normally enjoy
- speak to someone you trust

SLEEP

- keep a consistent sleep schedule
- create a short wind-down routine
- reduce stimulating activities before bed

ENJOYMENT

- start with small enjoyable activities
- avoid expecting yourself to fix everything immediately
- stay connected with supportive people

STRESS

- break large tasks into smaller steps
- take short breaks
- try slow breathing
- talk to someone if pressure becomes difficult to manage

SOCIAL CONNECTION

- message someone you trust
- spend time with supportive people
- consider speaking to a counsellor or trusted adult

These are general wellbeing suggestions.

Do not make medical claims.
